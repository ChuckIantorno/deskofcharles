About Me

My first entrepreneurial enterprise was leading an exterior home painting business. I was a freshman at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Through a company called College Works Painting, I managed the full life-cycle of operations: sales, hiring, training, and project execution. Although I had no prior painting experience, I finished the summer profitably. That’s when I knew I’d be an entrepreneur. Yes, that path may be harder and more risky, but I could not imagine doing it any other way.

My first full-time job after college was with a technology industry research and events company based in Palo Alto. My boss and I were responsible for business development and account management across the Americas. My boss was incredible, but she did not enjoy the details of contracts much. I quickly learned I had a talent for contract drafting and review, so the task of redlining with the other side came to me. I’d get the required signatures efficiently and effectively. I hit my sales targets consistently and learned a ton about the global technology industry.

After a few years of B2B sales, my entrepreneurial ambition was back in full force. I knew I had more to learn about how the world actually worked, and I felt eager to give back through my career. I decided to take the LSAT and pursue law school. I immediately felt at home at the George Mason University Antonin Scalia Law School, right outside of Washington, D.C.

There, my contracts professor advised me to take bankruptcy. That pulled me into real estate, insurance, and construction law. While in law school, I clerked for a law firm that solely defended architecture and engineering firms. They asked me to stay past the summer. Later, I worked under the General Counsel at the American Association of Port Authorities.

Since law school, I have advised clients on commercial contracts, disputes, trademarks, and real estate matters for small businesses. From June 2025 to June 2026, I was an in-house Risk Manager for a public works general contractor. I resolved private contract disputes, drafted public construction claims, and assisted outside counsel with litigation. I’ve had the privilege of working on construction disputes from the design side, the public-entity side, and the contractor’s side.

When I’m not working, I’m out hiking with Choco and Princess (pictured below), riding a bike, or reading by the beach. I care deeply about Los Angeles real estate and public works because there is so much opportunity waiting to be created. The City could be the greatest city in the world. Let’s build it!
