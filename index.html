Background

Business, Technology, Construction.

Before practicing law, I spent almost ten years managing my own businesses and working for small-to-medium sized enterprises. I grew up in the Chicago area and completed my undergraduate in Urbana-Champaign. Then, I went on to sell research, events, and web/mobile application development services in Silicon Valley During summers and a few semesters in law school, I worked under attorneys working in the public and private sectors in Virginia and DC. It was a privilege seeing how different businesses and different aspects of government work up close. In law school, I excelled in "Administrative Law" (constitutional law and statutory interpretation). My recent work has involved public works construction, real estate, trademarks, and enterprise contracts.
